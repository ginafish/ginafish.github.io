### Career

#### Cloud Software Engineer at Red Alpha, AWS ProServe Partner - 9/2024-Present

During my time at Red Alpha, I've been a partner for AWS ProServe. On ProServe engagements, I've been helping customers move from aging on-premise enterprise systems to fully cloud-native environments. This work has ranged from drafting migration plans to building full "production-ready prototypes".

#### Senior Software Engineer at Rupa Health - 12/2022-8/2024

Acquired by FullScript 10/2024

Rupa Health was a Series-A health technology startup. The company had a fantastic mission - to bring root-cause medicine to every person on the planet - and was fully remote, with employees across North and South America. I was a senior developer for the integrations team and a main maintainer of the company's DevOps infrastructure.

Snippets of what I did here:

* Championed and executed a software refactor that unlocked key business features, reduced friction with partners, and cut time spent manually reviewing results by 40%
* Fully owned end-to-end development for new critical features
* Onboarded and managed integrations with over a dozen partners, continuously building in resilience to integrations that handled over half the company's order volume
* Built dashboards and internal tooling for key internal stakeholders and operations team members
* Mentored other developers on infrastructure best practice
* Assisted other developers with system design for their projects - my favorite part of the job!
* Built UI experiences in React, APIs in Django/Python, and serverless automations in AWS Lambda

#### Software Engineer at Asymmetrik - 7/2018-11/2022

Acquired by BlueHalo 11/2021

This was my first full time and first software engineer role. I learned everything from how to build prototypes to how to refactor aging production systems that are struggling to keep the lights on, while balancing stakeholder wants and needs.
